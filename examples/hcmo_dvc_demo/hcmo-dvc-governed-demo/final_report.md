# Governed two-cage DVC fixture

## Question

What is the equal-cage mean activity in this synthetic interval after
per-cage aggregation to a common five-minute grid?

## Result

Cage A has a common-grid mean of 14 counts and cage B has a common-grid mean
of 22 counts. Giving each physical cage equal weight yields 18 counts.

## Scope

This result describes two synthetic cages over a deliberately tiny fixture
interval. It demonstrates trace reconciliation, cage-first aggregation, and
evidence lineage. It is not a complete biological day, a circadian profile, a
population estimate, or evidence about real animals.
